Mondo Assistant - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Mondo Assistant ("the Skill") is an Alexa skill that answers questions using an artificial intelligence service. This policy explains what happens to the information you provide when you use the Skill.

What the Skill receives

When you ask the Skill a question, Alexa sends the text of what you said to the Skill. The Skill does not ask for, and does not receive, your name, email address, phone number, address, payment details or any other account information.

How your question is used

The text of your question is forwarded to a third-party AI service (hub.aitech.asia) solely to generate an answer, which is then spoken back to you by Alexa. Please do not say sensitive personal information (for example passwords, financial or health details) to the Skill, because your words are sent to that service.

What we store

The Skill does not keep a database of your questions or answers and does not build user profiles. Basic technical logs (such as error messages and request timing) may be kept for a limited time by the hosting provider, Amazon Web Services, for troubleshooting.

Advertising, purchases and children

The Skill contains no advertising, does not offer purchases, and is not directed to children under 13.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Your questions are shared only with the AI service described above, as needed to answer you, and with Amazon as part of operating Alexa. Amazon's handling of your voice data is governed by the Amazon Alexa Terms of Use and Privacy Notice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
